#README 
The gene expression data (FPKM-UQ) hosted in this repository was obtained from The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA) program and accessed via the National Cancer Institute's Genomic Data Commons (GDC) Data Portal.

The results shown here are in whole or part based upon data generated by the TCGA Research Network: https://www.cancer.gov/tcga.

Data Access & Compliance

The datasets included in this repository fall under the GDC's Open Access tier. They consist of high-level, normalized gene expression quantifications that are completely unidentifiable and pose no patient privacy risks. All files meet the requirements for public redistribution.

References
